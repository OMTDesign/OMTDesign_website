3D printing

Precise prints that still work in service.

FDM printing with a select range of materials — ideal for prototyping, jigs, brackets, guards and small production runs in automotive, engineering and product design.

  • Affordable — cost-effective printed parts, priced by material and print time rather than guesswork.
  • Quick turnaround — items are printed and available as soon as we get them.
  • Build size — anything under 270 × 270 × 256 mm.
  • Multi-colour & multi-material — colour coding, flexible sections or toughened skins in a single print.
