3D scanning & scan-to-CAD

From a real part to an editable model.

A handheld 3D scanner captures existing parts as they are — worn originals, compound curves and one-off brackets that are hard to measure with callipers alone. We bring that scan into CAD and rebuild it as a clean, editable model, changes included: delete what you do not want, add what you do.

  • Handheld scanning of parts that exist — dash panels, trim, knobs, brackets and housings captured as-is, curves and all — no calliper marathon.
  • Scan-to-CAD — scanned surfaces rebuilt as editable solids and features: planes, cylinders, extrusions, ready to dimension.
  • "Delete" cut-outs — car dash-panel clean-ups: take out holes for unused gauges, switches or stereos, resurface where they were and cut what you want back.
  • Reverse engineering & 3D printing — scan a worn or obsolete part, redraw it clean, then print a like-for-like replacement.
